The sheet only offers the steps the rules allow at that moment: no "Proof validated" without proof pending, no "Proof requested" with nothing claimed, nothing at all once the case has ended.

Stages run beside the invoice steps, in order: SOA → Follow up → Collection letter. A locked stage shows on the sheet as a disabled chip with the reason. Stages never change the case status; the case screen shows them as a three-step track.

The three endings

Completed

Automatic. The moment every invoice in the case is Validated paid or Cleared.

  • Validated invoices appear under Validated, awaiting posting in Reports.
  • They leave that list by themselves once Accounting posts the payment and the balance reaches zero.

Not completed

The collector logs Ended: not completed, after a confirmation.

  • Open invoices stay marked Reconciliation and keep out of the regular bucket.
  • There is no reopen. To continue, open a new case on the account.

Escalated

The collector logs Escalated, after a confirmation.

  • The Collection Head gets one SMS with the client, open invoices and amount (Android only; on Windows it is just logged).
  • The Head takes the account on outside the app.
